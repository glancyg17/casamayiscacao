Our ceremonial cacao is from **Tabasco, Mexico**, the state next to Campeche.

## Where we get it

We buy it in the markets of Villahermosa, where harvests gather from cacao municipalities such as **Cárdenas, Comalcalco and Huimanguillo**, in the Chontalpa region. Then Mayis toasts and grinds it in Campeche.

We buy at the market, so we cannot point to one particular farm. What we can tell you is the region it comes from and how we work it, with nothing added.

## Why Tabasco

Tabasco is one of the states that produces the most cacao in Mexico, along with Chiapas. Cacao also has a very long history there: the region was home to the Chontal Maya, who already knew and traded cacao.

## How we work it

1. We buy the cacao in Villahermosa.
2. Mayis toasts it.
3. Mayis grinds it.
4. We shape it into a disc (30 and 100 g), a ball (200 g) or a block (500 g).

It is **100% cacao**, with no sugar or additives. Stored well, it keeps up to 12 months.
