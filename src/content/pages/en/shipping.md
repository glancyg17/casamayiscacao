## Shipping across Mexico

We ship with **Correos de México** anywhere in the country. You see the shipping cost when you place your order. When your package leaves, we let you know so you can track it.

To combine sizes in one shipment, message us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/529381341778) and we will put your order together.

## Pickup in Campeche

You can pick up your order at **Hacienda Santa María, Campeche**, Monday to Sunday, 9 am to 6 pm. Message us on WhatsApp so we have it ready.

## How to pay

On the [cacao page](/en/ceremonial-cacao/#sizes) you can pay online securely. If you prefer bank transfer or cash at pickup, message us.

## If something goes wrong

Because it is a food product, we do not accept returns. But if your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, send us a photo and we will make it right.

## Storage

Keep it tightly closed in a cool, dry place. It keeps up to 12 months.
