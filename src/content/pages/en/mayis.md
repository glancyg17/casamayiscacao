I am Mayis, from Campeche. I have spent a long time gathering people around cacao: in ceremonies, workshops and circles. Now I want you to be able to take home the same cacao I serve in them.

## Casa Mayis

Casa Mayis began as a wellness space. Today the heart of the house is cacao: I buy it in Tabasco, toast and grind it myself, and share it with you in your cup. The other services, such as massages, herbal baths and rituals, remain available on request. Write to me and we will talk.

## The thistle

The thistle in our logo is the emblem flower of Scotland. I have a special love for that country and its magical energy, which is why I chose it as the symbol of the house.

## The ceremonies

I hold cacao ceremonies, workshops and circles in Campeche. Everyone is welcome, and some circles are for women only. Dates are posted on Instagram, [@casamayis](https://instagram.com/casamayis).

Cacao is a tradition that comes from Mesoamerica. I practice it with respect and from my own experience, without speaking for anyone.
