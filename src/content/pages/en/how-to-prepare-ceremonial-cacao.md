Ceremonial cacao is easy to make. What changes is the pace: there is no hurry here. This is how Mayis prepares it at home.

## What you need (one cup)

- About 30 g of ceremonial cacao, as a reference. If you are new to pure cacao, start with less.
- One cup of water (about 250 ml).
- A cinnamon stick.
- Piloncillo or honey, if you want it sweet.
- A pinch of salt, if you like.
- A small pot, ideally clay, and a molinillo or balloon whisk.

## Step by step

1. **Chop or grate the cacao.** The smaller the pieces, the faster it dissolves.
2. **Warm the water with the cinnamon.** Until it is very hot, but not boiling.
3. **Add the cacao.** Stir with a wooden spoon until dissolved.
4. **Whisk with the molinillo.** Roll it between your palms to make foam. It is my favorite part.
5. **Sweeten to taste.** With piloncillo or honey, a little at a time.
6. **Serve hot and sip slowly.**

## Small tips

- **Do not let it boil.** It protects the flavor and texture.
- **If it is too thick,** add a little more hot water.
- **To accompany it,** try cardamom, vanilla or a touch of chili. Taste it plain first.
- **Set an intention.** Many people take a moment before drinking to think about what they want to give thanks for or let go of. It is optional, but I like it.

## Before and after

It is best on a light stomach. Drink water with it. If you take medication, are pregnant or have a health condition, check with your doctor first. And better not to mix it with alcohol or other substances.
